Shipped 2026-08-13 (#43). This file is the agreed design and stays here as the visual reference it was iterated into. The live page is now /bracket (src/cueclock/server/static/bracket.html), and where the two differ the live one is right — it computes its geometry from the field size, so it draws 3–16 players rather than the fixed eight sketched below. Three deliberate departures from these sketches, all made while building: (1) a match nobody has started shows a dash rather than 0, which from across the room reads as "lost 5–0"; (2) the connector from the B final to the final routes down, along the empty band between the two sides, and up — at sixteen the B final sits under the second final and a straight line would run through it; (3) the phone's race chip is dropped when a bracket match is loaded, because the match header above it already says the race. Keep iterating here for layout ideas; ship them in the live page.

Amended 2026-08-13 (live iteration, design.md §10.2). Three more places the live page has moved past these sketches, all from watching it rendered at 8 and 16 players: (4) a finished match says so through its winner and not through a caption — the box steps back, and the winning row keeps a white left edge, a lifted ground and both name and score bold white while the loser drops back. A word per state was built first and cut on sight: Final already means the A Final and the B Final, and Forfeit was the third place one small box said the same thing. · Live stays, because that one is about now rather than about a result. (5) a conceded match scores FF against whoever gave it up and W against the player who advanced — the language every bracket in every pool room already uses — and shows no race, because it never had one; (6) an estimated rating is prefixed ~, since the score bar refuses to show an unestablished number under a FARGO label and the two surfaces disagreeing is worse than either answer on its own.

Your question, and the one real design decision here. Short answer: hitting the target arms the result, it never fires it. One deliberate press commits. The free key at (3,5) — empty since the "I shot" key was pulled on 2026-08-05 — is where it lives, so no existing key changes meaning.

Why not auto-advance? You picked plus/minus scoring and called it the easiest to fat-finger. Auto-advance turns one stray press into a corrupted bracket that someone has to unwind mid-party. Arming costs one press and makes the failure mode a shrug.
